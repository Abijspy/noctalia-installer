A small release: Noctalia's owl is now your profile picture out of the box.

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Abijspy/noctalia-installer/v1.1.1/install.sh | bash
```

## New

- **Default profile picture:** the Noctalia logo becomes your avatar in Noctalia's launcher and Control Center and on the Noctalia Greeter login screen.
  - It's set through AccountsService (installed if missing) and as `~/.face`. Without AccountsService, Noctalia's `avatar_path` points at `~/.face`.
  - A picture you already have is kept. Change it any time in Noctalia's settings.
  - It's on the *Finishing touches* screen, or skip it with `--no-avatar`. `--uninstall` removes it and restores your AccountsService settings.
  - The logo is MIT-licensed by noctalia-dev and shipped in `configs/avatar/noctalia.png`.

## Checks

- 88 installer test scenarios, including keeping an existing profile picture.

Everything else is as in [v1.1.0](https://github.com/Abijspy/noctalia-installer/releases/tag/v1.1.0).
